Privacy Policy

Speed Reading: Schulte Table for iOS
Effective 12 September 2026 · Last updated 13 September 2026
The policy for whichever version of the app is current is always at safonov-a.github.io/privacy/schulte-table/ios.

In short: this app collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device. We have no servers that hold your data, and we cannot see your results.

Which version of the app this describes

This document describes the version of the app that brought you here. Each release links to the document written for it, and that link is fixed in the release, so you are reading about the app you actually have.

If a future release changes what the app stores or sends, it will not quietly change the meaning of this page: that release gets a document of its own and links there instead, while this one goes on describing the releases it was written for, for as long as they are in use.

Who we are

Speed Reading: Schulte Table (bundle identifier com.greenkeyuniverse.schultetable) is developed and published by Vadzim Niakhai, an independent developer, who is the data controller for the purposes of this policy. This policy covers the iOS version of the app only; the Android version is covered by a separate policy, because it is built on different services.

If you have any question about this policy or about your privacy, write to greenkeyuniverse@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We collect no personal information. The app does not ask you to create an account or to sign in, does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or health data, and does not read any of them.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following in its own private storage area on your device, so that it can work and so that your progress is not lost between sessions:

  • Your exercise results — completion times, dates, and which table layout each result belongs to.
  • Your progress through the course and through individual trainings.
  • Custom tables you create, including the name you give them.
  • Your settings — board size and mode, sound, vibration, font size, and whether you have already seen the introduction.

This information never leaves your device through the app. It is not uploaded, not synchronised, not shared and not sold. We have no way of reading it.

It is stored in the app's Documents folder, which means it may be included in your own iPhone or iPad backups — in iCloud or on your computer — if you have backups enabled. Those backups belong to you and are controlled by your Apple account settings, not by us.

Purchases

The app offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase that unlocks premium features. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see and never receive your payment card, billing address or Apple Account details.

To know whether premium is active, the app asks StoreKit on your device which purchases you currently own. That check happens locally, between the app and Apple's frameworks on your device; no result of it is sent to us.

Apple's own handling of your purchase is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics or usage statistics of any kind.
  • No crash or performance reporting.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No tracking, as Apple defines it — your data is never linked with data from other companies' apps or websites. The app therefore never asks for permission to track you.
  • No third-party software development kits that collect data.

Network use

The app does not send your results or any information about you over the network. Network activity is limited to:

  • Apple's App Store services, when you make a purchase, restore a purchase, or when the app asks Apple to show the standard "rate this app" prompt;
  • opening a link — this policy, or the app's App Store page — which hands the address to your browser or to the App Store app and leaves our app.

If you write to us

The app offers a "contact the developer" entry. Choosing it opens your own mail app with a new message addressed to us, pre-filled with the app's version and build number, your iOS version, and your interface language — nothing else. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

When you do send it, we receive your message and the email address you sent it from, and we use them for one purpose only: to answer you. We keep such correspondence no longer than we need in order to deal with your request, and we do not use it for anything else.

Links to other services

The app can open this policy in your browser and the app's page in the App Store. Those are operated by others, under their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their content or their practices.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included — there is nothing for us to collect.

Your rights

Privacy law in your region, including the General Data Protection Regulation in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, gives you rights over personal data a company holds about you: to see it, correct it, delete it, restrict its use, and object to its use.

We hold no personal data about you, so in practice there is nothing for us to hand over, correct or erase — with one exception: if you have written to us by email, you can ask us to delete that correspondence, and we will.

To erase everything the app has stored on your device, delete the app. iOS removes the app's storage with it, and your results, progress, custom tables and settings are gone. Your purchase is held by Apple against your Apple Account, not by us, and can be restored if you install the app again.

Changes to this policy

We may correct or clarify this page — a contact detail changes, or something turns out to be described imprecisely. Corrections apply to the app versions named above, and the date at the top is updated when we make one.

What we will not do is repurpose this page to describe a different release. A version of the app that changes what it stores or sends gets a document of its own, and links to it from inside the app, so no one is ever shown a description that does not match the software on their device.

Every change to this page is recorded publicly, with its date, in the repository it is published from: github.com/safonov-a/privacy.
